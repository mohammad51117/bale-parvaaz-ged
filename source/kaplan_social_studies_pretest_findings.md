## Part II map confirmation
Visual inspection of PDF pages 9–10 confirms that the Colonial Triangular Trade map shows a route from the British colonies to the West Indies, from the West Indies to Africa, and from Africa back to the British colonies. The map labels rum, gold, slaves, and molasses along the routes. Questions 1 and 2 use this single map context; the correct three sides for Question 1 are therefore the route in choice B, while Question 2 asks for the conclusion that cargo ships never had to sail empty on any leg of a profitable triangular journey.
